Dear Anu ♡

Hey Anuuu, I know you're upset with me, and I'm sorry for my part in that. I don't want to dismiss how you feel or make excuses instead of trying to understand. I know that saying sorry is only the beginning. What matters is listening properly, taking your feelings seriously and making a real effort to do better. You matter as a person, and your feelings deserve kindness and respect. I want us to be able to talk honestly, even when a conversation is difficult. You don't have to forgive me immediately, and you don't need to pretend you're happy just to make things easier. When you're ready, I'd like to hear what hurt you and understand it properly. I made this little page hoping it might bring a tiny smile to your day. The cats are very proud of their work, by the way. 🐈 Take your time, Anu. I hope we can work through this with patience and care, one step at a time. 🌷

with care, always ♡
